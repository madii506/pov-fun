Where you'd see it go wrong

  • A promo is missing: the wallet's balance drops with no receipt here.
  • TikTok rejects a video: the receipt says rejected, and the budget rolls to the next hour.
  • Volume stops: the fees stop, so the promos stop. Nothing else pays for them.
FAQ

The obvious questions.

Are these real TikToks?

Yes. Every video in the feed is a public TikTok, played with TikTok's own embed player. Open any of them on TikTok from the rail. The creators have nothing to do with POV.

Is this an ad for a memecoin on TikTok?

No. TikTok doesn't allow crypto ads, so POV's own videos are just memes: no ticker, no address, no price. The coin pays for the promotion; the videos never mention it.

Where does the money come from?

Only from $POV's creator fees, which every buy and sell pays on pump.fun. They land in the ad wallet, and that wallet is the whole budget.

How often does it promote?

Every hour there's budget to spend. A quiet hour rolls its budget into the next one.

Who writes POV's videos?

Holders do. Pick a format, write the caption and post it on X. The most-liked script each day becomes the next meme.

What do holders get?

Reach. Every promo puts POV's memes in front of people who have never heard of it. There are no payouts, and nothing here is a promise of returns.

Is POV part of TikTok?

No. POV pays for TikTok Promote like any account can. It has no deal with TikTok.
