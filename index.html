02 · HOW IT WORKS

TikTok sells the For You page. POV buys it with its own fees.

  1. Trades pay a fee

    Every $POV buy and sell on pump.fun pays a creator fee, and it goes to one address: the ad wallet.

    CHECK IT: every trade is public on Solana

  2. One wallet fills

    The ad wallet holds those fees and nothing else. Its balance is read live on this site.

    CHECK IT: the address, here and on Solscan

  3. Every hour: Promote

    Once an hour, a person moves that hour's budget to TikTok and presses Promote on one of POV's videos.

    CHECK IT: the transfer out of the wallet

  4. The receipt lands here

    The video, the budget, and what TikTok reported back: views, likes, follows.

    CHECK IT: the receipt, linked to both

TikTok's ad rules don't allow crypto ads, so the videos never mention the coin. The coin only pays for them.

04 · EVERY HOUR

Every hour, on the hour.

  1. :00, the wallet is read

    Whatever came in since the last promo is this hour's budget.

  2. Too little? It rolls over

    If the budget is too small for a promo, it carries into the next hour, so quiet hours still add up.

  3. The video is picked

    The most-liked script from the day, made into a video.

  4. Promote is pressed

    By a person, in the TikTok app, with that hour's budget.

  5. The receipt goes up

    Right away, then updated with TikTok's numbers when the promo ends.

09 · WHO PRESSES PROMOTE

A person. On the record.

TikTok Promote is a button in the TikTok app, paid for in the app, so a person runs it. They move the hour's budget from the ad wallet to TikTok and press Promote on the chosen video.

Everything they do leaves a trace you can check: the transfer out of the wallet on Solana, and the receipt on this page.

Where you'd see it go wrong

  • A promo is missing: the wallet's balance drops with no receipt here.
  • TikTok rejects a video: the receipt says rejected, and the budget rolls to the next hour.
  • Volume stops: the fees stop, so the promos stop. Nothing else pays for them.
10 · FAQ

The obvious questions.

Is this an ad for a memecoin on TikTok?

No. TikTok doesn't allow crypto ads, so POV's videos are just memes: no ticker, no address, no price. The coin pays for the promotion; the videos never mention it.

Where does the money come from?

Only from $POV's creator fees, which every buy and sell pays on pump.fun. They land in the ad wallet, and that wallet is the whole budget.

How often does it promote?

Every hour there's budget to spend. A quiet hour rolls its budget into the next one.

Who writes the videos?

Holders do. Pick a format, write the caption and post it on X. The most-liked script each day becomes the next video.

What do holders get?

Reach. Every promo puts POV's memes in front of people who have never heard of it. There are no payouts, and nothing here is a promise of returns.

Why does a person press Promote?

Promote lives in the TikTok app and is paid for there, so a person runs it. Every step leaves a trace: the wallet transfer on Solana and the receipt here.

What if TikTok rejects a video?

The receipt says rejected, and that hour's budget rolls into the next one.

Is POV part of TikTok?

No. POV pays for TikTok Promote like any account can. It has no deal with TikTok.
